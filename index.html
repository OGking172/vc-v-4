VC Custom Homes

At VC Custom Homes, we combine time-honored craftsmanship and quality with modern innovation and technology. Our knowledge, experience, and commitment to ongoing education mean you gain more when you choose us. Our goal is to maximize your investment while creating a home that truly fits the way you live.

We understand your home is the center of your world, so we design convenient, accessible, and inviting spaces with care and creativity.

Building a home can be stressful - we work hard to make the process enjoyable and straightforward.

While some builders may look for ways to inflate costs, our priority is that you see us as people of character and integrity at every step of the way.

Building with integrity

The journey to home.

VC Custom Homes specializes in design/build custom homes throughout the Upstate of South Carolina. We serve Greenville, Anderson, Oconee, Pickens, and Spartanburg counties, with projects in communities including Greenville, Anderson Pendleton, Seneca, Spartanburg, and Williamston, Travelers Rest as well as the Lake Hartwell and Lake Keowee areas. If you’re unsure whether we build in your specific location, contact us anytime—we’re happy to discuss your project.

Our process is built around you. You’ll work with a dedicated design consultant who guides you through creating the framework, exterior, and interior of your home so the result reflects your vision. We’re also here to answer your questions and walk you through every stage of building a custom home.
