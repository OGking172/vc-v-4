About us

Our legacy of quality

For more than 25 years, we’ve built custom and spec homes throughout Upstate South Carolina with one guiding principle: treat every home like it’s our own.

Crafting homes that fit your budget

Your vision. Your budget.

Building your dream home doesn’t have to come with a sky-high price tag. We take pride in offering a range of options that allow you to create a home that matches your needs, vision, and budget. From carefully sourced materials to thoughtful designs, we’ll help you get more for your money without compromising on the things that matter most.
